Tampa Home · Tear-Off & Re-Roof

Tin Roof Off, New Shingles in Two Days

We pulled off an old tin roof on a Thursday morning, repaired the rotted framing and laid new plywood, then had the shingles finished by early Friday afternoon, with the yard raked clean.

  • Rotted 2x4s replaced, new plywood
  • Roof vents and peel-and-stick underlayment
  • Debris cleaned up, yard raked

Sunroom · Flat Roof

A Leaking Sunroom, Finally Dry

A flat sunroom roof had leaked for over a year. Ricky and his grandson put a brand-new roof on it through the hot, humid days, and backed the work with a one-year warranty.

  • New flat roof over the sunroom
  • One-year workmanship warranty
  • Family crew: Ricky and his grandson
